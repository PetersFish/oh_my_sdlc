## Objective
Fix two review-agent blockers from the previous apply_change review:
1. Handoff history copying was limited to successful unblocked results; failed/blocked attempts were not preserved.
2. `eval_passed_or_human_decision_recorded` verification-basis guard could be satisfied by review-agent self-reported evidence without prior test-agent verification.

## Work Completed
### Blocker 1: Handoff History Preservation
- **Root cause**: `cmd_after_dispatch` in workflow.py only called `_write_handoff_history_copy` when `phase == "apply_change" and agent_status == "success" and not agent_blockers`.
- **Fix**: Removed the `agent_status == "success" and not agent_blockers` condition so handoff history is preserved for ALL apply_change worker results (success, failed, blocked) when `artifacts.handoff_path` exists.
- **Files changed**: `.ai/workflows/scripts/workflow.py`, `skills/sdlc-project-bootstrap/templates/workflow/workflow.py`

### Blocker 2: Verification-Basis Guard
- **Root cause**: The verification-basis guard checked `phase_evidence_view.get("verification_passed") or phase_evidence_view.get("regression_passed")`, which included the current review-agent's own evidence via the merged view.
- **Fix**: Changed the guard to check for prior successful test-agent evidence in `state.evidence.agent_results[slice_id]["test-agent"]`. Now requires:
  - `prior_test_agent.get("status") == "success"`
  - AND (`prior_test_agent.get("evidence", {}).get("verification_passed")` OR `prior_test_agent.get("evidence", {}).get("regression_passed")`)
- **Files changed**: `.ai/workflows/scripts/workflow.py`, `skills/sdlc-project-bootstrap/templates/workflow/workflow.py`
